## ✨ Features

- 📌 **Examination Overview & Pattern:** Quick breakdown of marks, duration, and subject weightage.
- 📄 **Previous Years Question Papers:** Instant view & download options for official past papers (Jan 2025, Oct 2025, May 2024, Aug 2023, Nov 2022).
- 🖼️ **Embedded PDF Viewer:** Preview question papers directly on the web page without leaving the portal.
- 💻 **CS/IS & RM Detailed Syllabi:** Interactive accordion lists covering 10 detailed units per subject along with curated reference books.
- 📊 **Progress Tracker Bar:** Track completed units with interactive checkboxes; updates automatically.
- 🔍 **Live Search & Filter:** Quickly find specific topics, concepts, or textbook titles across the entire syllabus.
- 📝 **Personal Study Notes:** A built-in scratchpad to jot down formulas and reminders, auto-saved locally on your device.
- 🌓 **Dark / Light Mode:** High-contrast themes for night-time reading and study sessions.
- 📱 **100% Mobile Responsive:** Optimized touch targets and responsive layouts tailored for mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.
